📋 More info

Crunchy Cakes — an independent label built for artists, not against them. No tricky contracts, no hidden fees.

💰 How royalties work

After distribution fees, we split net royalties 70% to you, 30% to the label. Payments processed via our distribution partner LabelCaster.

🖼️ Cover art

We provide a professional cover for every release — custom design, high resolution, ready for streaming platforms. If you already have cover art, that's fine too.

🌍 Distribution

We release to all major platforms through LabelCaster. Standard moderation takes about 7 days.

🔓 No exclusivity

You're free to release music anywhere else. No lock-in period.

🚫 What we don't do

  • No paid promo — we don't buy streams or fake placements
  • No guarantees — we don't promise a specific number of streams
  • No hidden fees — what you see is what you get
